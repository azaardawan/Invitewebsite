# Kurdish: everything still to check (complete list)

This file has **every piece of customer-facing text that still has no approved Kurdish**, in one place
and numbered. When these are approved, the customer website, the invitations, the printable card,
the keepsake PDF and the WhatsApp messages are fully in Kurdish. The admin panel is Arabic/English
only, so it isn't here. What is already approved (batches 1–6 and 8 in `KURDISH_REVIEW.md`) isn't
repeated.

**How to reply:** "approve all", or "approve all except …", and give your wording for those numbers
(say S for Sorani, B for Badini, or both). Nothing here is live until you approve it. Until then
Kurdish pages show the Arabic.

**Keep exactly as they are:** words in `{curly}` brackets (they are filled in automatically: a name, a
price, a link) and the `<terms>…</terms>` / `<refund>…</refund>` markers (they make those words a link;
put them around your translated words).

Confidence: **●** fairly confident · **◐** please check · **○** unsure, likely needs your wording.

Words I used, taken from your earlier approvals: brand **بەهجە**; invitation **بانگهێشتنامە** (S) /
**داخوازنامە** (B); page **پەرە** (S) / **لاپەر** (B); link **بەستەر** (S) / **لینک** (B); guest
**میوان** (S) / **مێڤان** (B); order **داواکاری** (S) / **داخوازی** (B); congratulations
**پیرۆزبایی** (S) / **پیرۆزباهی** (B); "please" **تکایە** (S) / **هیڤییە** (B).
